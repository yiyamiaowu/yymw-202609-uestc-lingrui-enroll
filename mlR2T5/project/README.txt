如果要运行我的代码，请将解压好的图片放在data文件夹中
命令行工具可以使用如下指令
[-h] --data_dir DATA_DIR [--model {baseline,unet,derainnet,mymodel}] [--mode {train,test}]
               [--checkpoint CHECKPOINT] [--epochs EPOCHS] [--batch_size BATCH_SIZE] [--lr LR] [--use_perceptual_loss]
               [--lambda_pixel LAMBDA_PIXEL] [--lambda_perceptual LAMBDA_PERCEPTUAL] [--use_easy_contrastive_loss]
               [--lambda_easy_contrastive LAMBDA_EASY_CONTRASTIVE] [--use_infoNCE_loss]
               [--lambda_infoNCE_loss LAMBDA_INFONCE_LOSS] [--use_attention_loss]
               [--lambda_attention LAMBDA_ATTENTION]
